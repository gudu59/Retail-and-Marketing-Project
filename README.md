
# 🛍️ Retail & Marketing Analytics Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on using **data analytics and customer behavior analysis** to understand retail sales, identify valuable customer segments, evaluate product performance, and support better marketing decisions.

The project covers the complete analytics process — from **data cleaning and exploratory analysis to customer segmentation, KPI analysis, and dashboard development**.

**Domain:** Retail, E-commerce & Marketing Analytics  
**Project Type:** End-to-End Data Analytics Project

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A multi-channel retail company is facing challenges such as:

- Declining customer retention
- Inconsistent sales across product categories
- Inefficient marketing spending
- Limited understanding of customer behavior

The objective is to use data to help the business:

- Identify high-value customers
- Improve customer retention
- Optimize marketing campaigns
- Understand product and regional performance
- Increase Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Reduce Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

---

## 🚀 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Perform **customer segmentation** using RFM analysis and clustering.
2. Analyze **sales trends and product performance**.
3. Evaluate **customer behavior and purchasing patterns**.
4. Calculate important **business KPIs**.
5. Analyze customer lifetime value and retention.
6. Build interactive dashboards for business users.
7. Generate actionable recommendations from the analysis.

## 👥 Customer Segmentation

### RFM Analysis

Customers are analyzed using:

- **Recency** – How recently a customer purchased
- **Frequency** – How often a customer purchases
- **Monetary** – How much a customer spends

Customers are then categorized into groups such as:

- Champions
- Loyal Customers
- Potential Loyalists
- New Customers
- At-Risk Customers
- Lost Customers

This helps the business create targeted marketing strategies for different customer groups.

### K-Means Clustering

K-Means clustering is also applied to RFM variables to identify distinct customer groups.

The **Elbow Method** and **Silhouette Score** are used to determine an appropriate number of clusters.

---

## 🛒 Advanced Analytics

The project includes several advanced analytics techniques:

### Market Basket Analysis

Association rules are used to identify products that are frequently purchased together.

This can support:

- Product bundling
- Cross-selling
- Upselling
- Recommendation strategies

### Cohort Analysis

Cohort analysis is used to study customer retention over time and understand how different groups of customers continue purchasing after their first purchase.

### Customer Lifetime Value

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) is calculated to estimate the potential value of customers over their relationship with the business.
